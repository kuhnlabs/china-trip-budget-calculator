Methodology & Pricing Notes

Accommodation Pricing

Estimates are based on double occupancy for couples and families. Backpacker tiers reflect comfortable youth hostels in major centers (Beijing, Shanghai) and local guesthouses. Mid-range tiers reflect verified 4-star hotels with private bathrooms and English reception.

Food & Dining

Budget assumes local noodle bars, dumplings, and street stalls (¥30–¥60/day). Mid-range includes sit-down regional specialties (Peking Duck, Sichuan hotpot, dim sum) with tea and drinks (¥120–¥250/day).

High-Speed Rail & Transport

Calculated based on standard China Railway 2nd/1st class ticket rates for standard tourist corridors (e.g., Beijing to Xi'an ~$80, Xi'an to Chengdu ~$40).

Tibet Autonomous Region (TAR)

Tibet requires pre-arranged private guides, designated tourist vehicles, and official TTB permits by Chinese regulations, which creates a higher baseline cost than mainland coastal provinces.
